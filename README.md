MassMutual Brand Studio
=======================

A simple, Canva-style design tool that only uses official MassMutual brand assets:
the brand colors, the 432 MassMutual icons, the logos, brand fonts, dot patterns
and on-brand templates (Teams backgrounds, slides, social posts, banners, flyers,
certificates and more).

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
1. Double-click index.html. It opens in Edge or Chrome.
   No installation, no login and no internet needed.
2. Pick a template (or a blank size), change the words and photos, then click
   Download.

HOW TO SHARE IT WITH COLLEAGUES
-------------------------------
- Zip the whole BrandStudio folder and send it, or put the folder on a Teams or
  SharePoint site.
- Keep the folder together. index.html needs the assets, css and js folders
  next to it.
- Designs save automatically in each person's own browser (see "My designs").
- To hand an editable design to someone else, use Download > Save design file
  (.mmdesign). They open it from My designs > Open a design file.

TIPS
----
- Double-click text to type. Double-click a photo to replace it.
- Drag photos from your computer straight onto the page or into a photo frame.
- The pill in the top bar is the brand check. It flags hard-to-read text, the
  wrong logo color, logos that are too small or crowded, and accent colors used
  too heavily. Click it to see the list, then click an item to jump to it.
- Download > Copy image lets you paste straight into Teams, Outlook or PowerPoint.
- Print sizes (flyer, certificate) export at 300 dpi.
- Custom size: Templates tab > Custom size (pixels or inches). To change the
  size of the design you're on, click an empty part of the page and use
  Page size on the right ("Scale my items" grows or shrinks everything).
- Download > PNG transparent removes the page background. Download > Selected
  items only saves just what you've selected, cropped, with no background.
- Photo > Remove background cuts a person or object out of a plain backdrop.
  Use the Strength slider to fine-tune, or Restore original to undo.

EMAIL SIGNATURE
---------------
Signature tab: just start typing. The signature appears on the page and
updates live as you type or change the layout, logo (MMSD, MassMutual or
MassMutual 175 Years), style or photo. Pieces you moved or resized stay where
you put them; "Start over" resets to the official layout. Every piece (photo,
name, button, logo, background) can be moved, resized or deleted. To make the
banner longer or shorter, click an empty spot and change Page size.
Download > Copy image, then paste it in Outlook: File > Options > Mail >
Signatures. If you added a button link, the pasted signature is clickable.

EMAIL BUILDER
-------------
Email (left bar) builds a branded MassMutual email from the official Email
Builder modules, in the approved order. Pick a template and brand, fill in the
boxes or click words in the preview to type there, add or reorder sections.
- Outlook draft (.eml): opens in Outlook as a new message, ready to send.
- Copy for Outlook: paste into a new Outlook email.
- Download .html / Copy HTML code: for Marketing or an email platform.
Compliance wording (disclosures, CRN, project number) must come from
Compliance. Anything left blank stays as a placeholder for them.

FONTS
-----
- Headlines use MM Sharp Sans and quotes use Freight Text Pro. These are
  licensed fonts, so they are NOT included. If they are installed on the
  computer (they usually are on MassMutual-imaged PCs), the app uses them
  automatically. If not, a close stand-in is shown and the Brand tab says so.
- Lato (body copy) is built in. Its free license is in assets\Lato-OFL.txt.

PHOTOS
------
The Photos tab includes the MassMutual stock library (about 1,075 Business,
Consumer and LinkedIn background photos). These are licensed for INTERNAL
MassMutual use only. Do not share them outside MassMutual, including with
financial professionals.
You can also use approved photography from the MassMutual Brand Library:
https://massmutual.sharepoint.com/sites/BrandLibrary/brandcenterdownloads/Forms/AllItems.aspx
Download a photo there and drop it into the Photos tab. Photos stay on your
computer and are never uploaded anywhere.

Brand questions: brandmanagement@MassMutual.com

FOR MAINTAINERS
---------------
- assets\icons.js, assets\logos.js and assets\lato.css are generated by
  tools\build_assets.py from the official icon deck (.pptx). Icon names and
  categories are in tools\icon_names.txt.
  The 175 Years logos come from tools\logo175\*.png (white = logo), cut from
  the official MM175 LinkedIn banner. If Brand sends a vector (SVG/EPS)
  version, replace them with it.
  Rebuild: python tools\build_assets.py "<path to icon deck .pptx>"
- js\render.js   drawing engine (used for the editor, thumbnails and exports)
- js\templates.js  page sizes, text styles and templates (add new ones here)
- js\app.js      the editor
- js\email.js    the email builder; js\voice.js  editorial-guide word checks
- assets\email.js is generated by tools\build_email.py from the Email Builder
  Agent modules in tools\email_src. Rebuild: python tools\build_email.py
- assets\photos\ is generated by tools\build_photos.py from assets\Business,
  assets\Consumer and assets\LinkedInBackgrounds (only new photos are
  re-encoded). Rebuild after adding photos: python tools\build_photos.py
  The original photo folders aren't needed to run the app, only to rebuild.
- assets\sig.js holds the signature background artwork.
